Your files in, the lists your CRM team needs out.

Three tools for a Shopify and Klaviyo store. Load your own exports, or try them on the example files. Everything runs in this page: your files never leave your computer.

Loading the analysis engine (about 10 seconds the first time)

Check a template before it goes live

Paste the HTML from Klaviyo's code editor. It finds what a recipient would see: a greeting that reads "Ciao ,", a dead link, a hand-typed UTM that breaks the naming rules, a missing unsubscribe link, a subject cut off on a phone.

Lifecycle stages, behind a consent gate

From the Shopify orders export (Orders > Export, as it comes) and the consent export of your sign-up form or ESP. Every customer gets a stage: New, Repeat, At risk, Dormant or Churned. Only people whose consent has a recorded source and date are kept; everyone else goes to a suppression list with the reason.

When to send the second-order email

From the same orders export: how many customers buy again by day 30, 60, 90, 180 and 365, counting only customers who had a full year to come back. The median is the delay to put in the post-purchase flow.

The page runs the repository's own Python code in your browser with Pyodide: the same functions and tests as the command line. Nothing is sent to Klaviyo from here; writing to Klaviyo is a command-line step with an API key. Example files are handwritten with example.com addresses.
